# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RuleTrip a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

- use welcoming and inclusive language;
- respect differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- focus criticism on claims, code, and evidence—not people;
- show empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and publishing others' private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Serious incidents may be reported through the private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
